Treatments

Complete dental care,
one coherent plan.

From routine check-ups to complex surgery, every treatment is designed for a functional, lasting and natural-looking result.

  • PeriodonticsGum disease treatment (gingivitis, periodontitis), deep cleaning and root planing, gum grafts for recessions, regenerative surgery and maintenance.
  • Dental implants & oral surgerySingle and multiple implants, computer-guided surgery, bone grafts and sinus lifts, full-arch fixed solutions (All-on-4 type), wisdom teeth and complex extractions.
  • Cosmetic dentistryPorcelain veneers, professional teeth whitening, bonded restorations, gum contouring and crown lengthening.
  • Crowns, bridges & denturesCeramic crowns and bridges, implant-supported prostheses and full-mouth rehabilitation, designed with digital impressions (CAD/CAM).
  • General dentistry & emergenciesCheck-ups, scaling and cleaning, fillings, root canal treatment, dental pain and emergencies.
  • Oral care & cancerDental assessment and support before, during and after cancer treatment, in coordination with your oncology team. Dr Richard Dahan is the founder and president of the French Society of Dentistry in Oncology (SFDC).

The practice

A calm setting in the heart of Saint-Germain.

Steps from the Carrefour de l'Odéon, the practice occupies a Parisian building on boulevard Saint-Germain. Its treatment rooms, filled with natural light and opening onto balconies, are designed for calm, discretion and precision. Established in the 6th arrondissement since 2001.

International visitors and expatriates are welcome. We provide detailed treatment plans and invoices for reimbursement by your insurance.

Do you speak English?
Yes. Consultations can be held in English, and treatment plans are explained in detail before any treatment begins.
Can I come for a dental emergency?
Please call the practice: we do our best to see patients in pain as quickly as possible during opening hours. Outside opening hours, call 15 (SAMU) or 112 in a medical emergency.
I am visiting Paris. Can I be reimbursed?
You will receive a detailed invoice (note d'honoraires) that you can submit to your insurer. Dr Richard Dahan is a sector 1 (conventionné) practitioner and the practice accepts the French Carte Vitale.
